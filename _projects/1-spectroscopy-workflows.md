I am developing a modular workflow for asking a chemically useful question: **how does a dopant or point defect change the spectrum we measure?**

The project is designed around VASP outputs and Python atomistic tooling, with Raman/IR lattice dynamics as an initial target and a longer-term path toward additional spectroscopy modules.

The emphasis is on reproducibility: standardised calculation inputs, validated post-processing, consistent plotting and direct comparison between pristine and defective structures.
