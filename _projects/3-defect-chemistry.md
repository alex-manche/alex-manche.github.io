Point-defect calculations provide a route from a nominal dopant composition to the actual microscopic defects likely to exist in a material. My workflow combines global structure searching around defects, thermodynamic corrections and chemical-potential analysis to resolve site preference, charge state and compensation mechanisms.

A recurring goal is to connect these predictions to experimentally relevant degradation or performance mechanisms such as oxygen vacancy formation, transition-metal redox and carrier localisation.
